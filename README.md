# Habit Tracker — Minimal, Fast, and Personal

A lightweight weekly habit-tracking app focused on **simplicity**, **speed**, and staying consistent.  
Track weekly habit hours, manage daily To-Dos, and avoid the clutter of overbuilt productivity apps.

This project’s goal is to provide an experience that is **simple**, **pleasant**, and lightly **gamified**, so habits feel rewarding—not tedious.

---

## Automated Reset System (Cron Jobs)

Two background cron jobs keep everything running smoothly and cleanly:

### **1. Daily To-Do Reset**
- **Runs:** Every day at **23:59:59**  
- **Purpose:** Clears your daily To-Dos so each morning starts fresh.

### **2. Weekly Habit Hours Reset**
- **Runs:** Every Sunday at **23:59:59**  
- **Purpose:** Archives your weekly progress and resets tracked hours for the new week.

---

## Project Goal

The goal is to build an extremely simple and enjoyable Habit Tracker—something that avoids the complexity of existing apps while still giving you structure, progress tracking, and a bit of fun through light gamification.

Designed for easy personal use.
